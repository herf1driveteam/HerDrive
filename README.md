# Her Drive F1

Her Drive F1 is a student-led digital platform created to highlight and amplify women in the Formula 1 community. The project aims to challenge stereotypes surrounding women in motorsport by showcasing stories, perspectives, and contributions from female fans and professionals in the F1 space.

## Purpose
Motorsport communities, particularly Formula 1, have historically lacked visible representation of women. Her Drive F1 addresses this gap by providing a centralized platform that celebrates women’s involvement in F1 and encourages inclusivity within the sport.

## My Role
I led the development and launch of the website, managing front-end implementation, content organization, and overall project direction. This included structuring pages, implementing responsive design, and coordinating outreach to feature stories on the platform.
